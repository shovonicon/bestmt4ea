## **What Is Buyside Liquidity?**

Buyside liquidity refers to clusters of buy stop orders placed above previous highs. Retail traders typically place stop-loss orders above swing highs when they sell short.

Institutions know this.

When price pushes above those highs, it triggers stop-loss orders and breakout entries. This creates a surge of buy orders — liquidity — that large players use to enter massive sell positions.

In short:

-   Buyside liquidity = Orders above highs
-   Often targeted before bearish reversals

* * *

## **What Is Sellside Liquidity?**

Sellside liquidity exists below previous lows. Traders place stop-losses under swing lows when they buy.

When price drops below those lows:

-   Stops get triggered
-   Panic selling occurs
-   Institutions absorb liquidity
-   Market reverses upward

This is called a liquidity sweep or stop hunt.

Understanding this concept changes how you see charts forever.
