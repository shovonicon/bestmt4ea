In Forex trading, indicators that don’t repaint are crucial for traders looking for reliable, consistent signals. A “non-repainting” indicator means that once the signal (arrow or any other signal) is generated, it doesn’t change its position or disappear as new data comes in. Here are some of the best non-repainting arrow indicators that you can use for Forex trading:

### 1\. **Arrow Trend Indicator**

-   **Type:** Trend-following indicator
-   **Functionality:** The Arrow Trend Indicator is designed to indicate whether the market is in an uptrend or downtrend. It uses a combination of moving averages and price action to place arrows above or below the price, showing potential buy (green arrow) or sell (red arrow) signals. These signals do not change once generated, making it a reliable tool for traders.
-   **Pros:**
    -   Simple and intuitive
    -   Non-repainting
    -   Ideal for identifying trends
-   **Cons:**
    -   It can lag in volatile markets

### 2\. **RSI Arrows Indicator**

-   **Type:** Momentum indicator
-   **Functionality:** The RSI Arrows Indicator uses the Relative Strength Index (RSI) to show arrows when the market is overbought or oversold. When RSI crosses above a certain threshold (e.g., 70), it shows a sell arrow, and when it crosses below a threshold (e.g., 30), it shows a buy arrow. Once an arrow appears, it doesn’t repaint.
-   **Pros:**
    -   Good for identifying overbought and oversold conditions
    -   Non-repainting signals
-   **Cons:**
    -   False signals in sideways markets

### 3\. **Supertrend Indicator**

-   **Type:** Trend-following indicator
-   **Functionality:** The Supertrend indicator plots arrows on the chart based on the average true range (ATR) and a multiplier. The indicator adjusts with volatility, and the arrows are drawn on the price when it crosses the calculated trend line. It’s particularly effective in trending markets and offers non-repainting signals.
-   **Pros:**
    -   Works well in trending markets
    -   Clear buy and sell signals
    -   Non-repainting
-   **Cons:**
    -   Can give false signals during sideways markets

### 4\. **MACD Arrows Indicator**

-   **Type:** Trend-following and momentum indicator
-   **Functionality:** The Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD) is one of the [most popular indicators](https://bestmt4ea.com/product/golden-deer-holy-grail-indicator/ "Golden Deer Holy Grail Indicator (Lifetime Premium)") for identifying market momentum and trend direction. The MACD Arrows Indicator adds arrows based on MACD crossovers: when the MACD line crosses above the signal line, a buy arrow is shown, and when it crosses below, a sell arrow is shown.
-   **Pros:**
    -   Combines trend and momentum signals
    -   Non-repainting
    -   Works in both trending and range-bound markets
-   **Cons:**
    -   Can be delayed in volatile conditions

### 5\. **Ichimoku Cloud Indicator**

-   **Type:** Trend-following and support/resistance indicator
-   **Functionality:** While not traditionally an “arrow” indicator, the Ichimoku Cloud can be used to identify buy and sell signals by looking for price action crossing the cloud or a signal line crossover. It is widely known for providing clear and non-repainting signals about the market trend and potential reversal points.
-   **Pros:**
    -   Provides comprehensive market analysis
    -   Non-repainting
    -   Great for spotting trends and reversals
-   **Cons:**
    -   Complex for beginners
    -   Signals may lag

### 6\. **TDI (Traders Dynamic Index) Indicator**

-   **Type:** Momentum and trend indicator
-   **Functionality:** The TDI combines elements of the RSI, moving averages, and volatility bands to create a versatile indicator. Arrows are generated when the TDI’s green line crosses the red line, indicating a buy or sell signal. These signals are non-repainting once they appear.
-   **Pros:**
    -   Good for both trending and ranging markets
    -   Non-repainting signals
-   **Cons:**
    -   Can be complex to interpret for beginners

### 7\. **Price Action Arrows**

-   **Type:** Price action-based indicator
-   **Functionality:** This indicator draws arrows based on key price action patterns like pin bars, engulfing candles, and inside bars. Once an arrow is drawn, it does not repaint or change its position.
-   **Pros:**
    -   Purely based on price action
    -   Non-repainting signals
-   **Cons:**
    -   Relies heavily on the quality of price action patterns, which can be subjective

Some indicators, like the **Arrow Trend Indicator**, **MACD Arrows**, and **Supertrend Indicator**, are great for clear trend-following signals, while the **RSI Arrows** and **TDI** can help spot momentum shifts.
