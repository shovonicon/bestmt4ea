## **What Is a Forex Expert Advisor (EA)?**

A Forex Expert Advisor (EA) is a software program that runs on MetaTrader platforms (MT4 or MT5). It follows programmed rules to automatically:

-   Analyze price charts
-   Identify trading signals
-   Open and close trades
-   Manage stop-loss and take-profit levels

You can learn more about how algorithmic trading works at [Investopedia](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/a/algorithmictrading.asp).

In short, an EA trades based on logic—not emotions.

# **How to Install and Set Up the EA**

## **MetaTrader Installation Guide**

1.  Open MetaTrader 4 or 5
2.  Go to “File” → “Open Data Folder”
3.  Paste the EA file into the “Experts” folder
4.  Restart MetaTrader
5.  Drag the EA onto a chart
6.  Enable AutoTrading

Simple—but critical.
