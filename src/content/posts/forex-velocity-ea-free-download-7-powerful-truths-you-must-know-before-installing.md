## **What Is an Expert Advisor (EA)?**

An Expert Advisor (EA) is an automated trading software used in MetaTrader 4 (MT4) or MetaTrader 5 (MT5). It follows predefined rules to open and close trades without human intervention.

### **How Automated Forex Robots Work**

EAs analyze:

-   Price action
-   Technical indicators
-   Market volatility
-   Pre-programmed conditions

Once conditions are met, the robot executes trades instantly. No hesitation. No emotion. Just code.

## **Installation Guide**

If you choose to install a legitimate version, here’s how:

### **Installing on MT4**

1.  Open MetaTrader 4
2.  Click “File” → “Open Data Folder”
3.  Navigate to MQL4 → Experts
4.  Paste the EA file
5.  Restart MT4
6.  Drag the EA onto a chart
7.  Enable AutoTrading

Make sure:

-   “Allow live trading” is enabled
-   DLL imports are allowed (if required)

## **Performance Analysis**

Before trusting any EA, analyze data carefully.

### **Understanding Backtest Results**

Key metrics include:

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Win Rate | Percentage of winning trades |
| Drawdown | Maximum account loss from peak |
| Profit Factor | Gross profit divided by gross loss |
| Sharpe Ratio | Risk-adjusted returns |

Be cautious of:

-   Unrealistic 99% win rates
-   Extremely low drawdowns
-   Short backtest periods

### **Real Market Conditions**

Backtests often ignore:

-   Slippage
-   Spread widening
-   Broker manipulation
-   News volatility

Real trading conditions can significantly reduce performance.
