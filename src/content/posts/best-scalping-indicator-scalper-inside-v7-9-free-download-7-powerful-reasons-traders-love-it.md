## **What Is Scalping in Trading?**

Scalping is a short-term trading strategy where traders aim to profit from small price movements. Instead of holding trades for hours or days, scalpers may hold positions for seconds or minutes.

### **How Scalping Differs from Day Trading**

While day traders close all positions before the market closes, scalpers operate at a much faster pace. They may execute dozens—or even hundreds—of trades in a single session.

Key differences:

-   Scalping targets smaller profits per trade
-   Higher trade frequency
-   Requires tight spreads and low commissions
-   Heavy reliance on technical indicators

### **Why Indicators Matter in Scalping**

Because price moves so quickly, manual analysis alone isn’t enough. Scalpers rely on:

-   Momentum indicators
-   Moving averages
-   Volume analysis
-   Signal-based algorithms

A reliable indicator helps eliminate emotional trading and guesswork.
