## **Introduction to Order Blocks in Forex Trading**

Order blocks have become extremely popular among price action traders. They help identify areas where banks and institutions place large orders. Instead of guessing where price might reverse, traders use order blocks to trade with institutional momentum.

### **What Are Order Blocks?**

Order blocks are specific price zones where large buy or sell orders were previously placed. These areas often cause strong reactions in the market when revisited.

Simply put:

-   A **bullish order block** forms before a strong upward move.
-   A **bearish order block** forms before a strong downward move.

When price returns to these zones, it often reacts again.

### **Why Institutional Traders Use Order Blocks**

Large financial institutions cannot place all their trades at once. They build positions gradually. This leaves “footprints” on the chart—what we call order blocks.

Retail traders who understand this concept gain a serious edge. Instead of trading randomly, they follow smart money.
