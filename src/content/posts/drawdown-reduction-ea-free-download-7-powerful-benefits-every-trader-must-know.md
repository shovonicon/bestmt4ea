## **What Is a Drawdown in Forex Trading?**

Before discussing **Drawdown Reduction EA Free Download**, it’s important to understand what drawdown actually means.

In Forex trading, drawdown refers to the reduction in account balance from a peak to a trough. It shows how much your account has decreased before recovering.

### **Types of Drawdown**

| Type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Absolute Drawdown | Difference between initial deposit and lowest point |
| Relative Drawdown | Percentage drop from peak balance |
| Maximum Drawdown | Largest observed loss from peak to bottom |

High drawdown can destroy trader confidence and even wipe out accounts.
