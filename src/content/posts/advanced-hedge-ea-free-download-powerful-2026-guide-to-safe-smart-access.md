## **The Core Concept of Hedging in Forex**

Hedging is a strategy used to reduce risk. In forex trading, hedging often means opening opposite positions on the same currency pair to protect against sudden price movements.

For example:

-   You open a BUY position.
-   If price moves against you, a SELL position is opened to offset losses.
