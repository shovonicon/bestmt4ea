## **What Is a No Repaint Indicator?**

A no repaint indicator is a technical analysis tool that does not modify or remove trading signals once the candle closes. In contrast, repainting indicators adjust past signals based on new market data, which can create misleading backtesting results.

In simple terms:

-   **Repainting indicator:** Changes past signals
-   **No repaint indicator:** Keeps signals fixed after confirmation

This stability builds trust and helps traders evaluate performance accurately.
