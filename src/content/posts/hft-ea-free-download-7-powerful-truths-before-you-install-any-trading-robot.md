## **What Is an HFT EA?**

An **HFT EA** (High-Frequency Trading Expert Advisor) is an automated trading software used mainly on platforms like MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5.

It executes:

-   Multiple trades per minute
-   Very small profit targets (scalping)
-   Lightning-fast entries and exits
-   Automated risk management rules

HFT systems rely heavily on:

-   Low latency execution
-   VPS hosting
-   Tight spreads
-   High liquidity brokers

Unlike manual traders, HFT EAs use algorithms to spot micro price movements. These movements may be tiny—but when repeated hundreds of times, they can add up.

However, that’s the theory. Reality can be different.
