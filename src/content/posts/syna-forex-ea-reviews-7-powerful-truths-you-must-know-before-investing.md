## **What Is a Forex Expert Advisor (EA)?**

A Forex Expert Advisor (EA) is a software program designed to automate trading decisions on platforms like MetaTrader 4 (MT4) or MetaTrader 5 (MT5). Instead of placing trades manually, the EA scans the market, identifies trading opportunities, and executes buy or sell orders based on predefined rules.

Think of it as a trading assistant that never sleeps.

EAs typically rely on:

-   Technical indicators
-   Price action patterns
-   Trend analysis
-   Risk management algorithms

However, not all EAs are created equal — and that’s where careful research becomes critical.
