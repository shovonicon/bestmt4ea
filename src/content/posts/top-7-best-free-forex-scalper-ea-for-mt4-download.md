# **How to Install the Best Free Forex Scalper EA on MT4**

Installing a scalping EA is easier than you think.

* * *

## **Downloading and Adding Files to MT4**

1.  Download the EA (.ex4 or .mq4 file).
2.  Open MT4.
3.  Click **File → Open Data Folder**.
4.  Navigate to **MQL4 → Experts**.
5.  Paste the EA file.
6.  Restart MT4.

* * *

## **Activating the EA on a Chart**

1.  Open MT4’s **Navigator** pane.
2.  Drag the EA onto your chart.
3.  Enable **AutoTrading** and allow DLL imports if required.
4.  Configure your settings.

* * *

## **Testing with MT4 Strategy Tester**

The built-in tester lets you backtest your EA on historical data. This helps you adjust settings and see how the EA would have performed in the past.
