Scalping is a short-term trading strategy where traders aim to capture small price changes. Instead of holding trades for hours or days, scalpers enter and exit within minutes — sometimes seconds.

The goal? Small, frequent profits that add up over time.

Scalping usually happens on:

-   1-minute charts
-   5-minute charts
