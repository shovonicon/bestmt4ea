## **How Correlation Hedging Works in Forex**

Correlation hedging relies on the statistical relationship between currency pairs.

### **Understanding Currency Pair Correlation**

Correlation measures how two currency pairs move in relation to each other:

-   **Positive correlation (+1):** Both pairs move in the same direction.
-   **Negative correlation (-1):** Pairs move in opposite directions.
-   **Zero correlation (0):** No predictable relationship.

For example, EUR/USD and GBP/USD often move in similar directions because both involve the US dollar.

### **Why Traders Use Hedging Strategies**

Hedging helps traders:

-   Reduce overall risk exposure
-   Limit drawdowns
-   Protect capital during volatility
-   Balance open positions

Instead of relying on one directional trade, correlation hedging spreads the risk intelligently.
