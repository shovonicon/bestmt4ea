## 📊 What Is a Currency Strength Meter Indicator?

4

A **currency strength meter indicator** measures the relative strength of individual currencies like USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, and others.

Instead of analyzing just one pair (like EUR/USD), it evaluates all major pairs and calculates how strong or weak each currency is across the market.

### 🔎 Why It Matters

Forex pairs move because one currency is stronger than the other.

Example:

-   If USD is strong
-   And EUR is weak  
    ➡ EUR/USD is likely to fall.

This is why many professional traders rely on strength meters as part of their trading systems.

* * *

## ⚙️ How Does a Currency Strength Meter Work?

A strength meter analyzes multiple currency pairs and calculates momentum using:

-   Moving averages
-   RSI calculations
-   Price changes
-   Volatility comparisons
-   Relative performance ranking

Most indicators show strength values on a scale like:

| Strength Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 7–8 | Very Strong |
| 5–6 | Strong |
| 3–4 | Neutral |
| 1–2 | Weak |
| 0 | Very Weak |

The key idea is simple:

👉 **Buy the strongest currency.**  
👉 **Sell the weakest currency.**
