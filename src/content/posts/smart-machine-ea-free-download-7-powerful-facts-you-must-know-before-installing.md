## **What Is Smart Machine EA?**

Smart Machine EA is an automated trading software designed to operate on MetaTrader platforms. It analyzes market conditions and executes trades automatically based on coded strategies.

In simple terms? It’s like having a robot trader working 24/7.

### **How Expert Advisors Work in MetaTrader**

Expert Advisors are scripts written in MQL (MetaQuotes Language). They:

-   Analyze price movements
-   Identify trading signals
-   Open and close trades
-   Manage stop-loss and take-profit levels
-   Monitor market conditions continuously

Once installed on MT4 or MT5, the EA runs automatically as long as your trading terminal is active.

### **Algorithmic Trading Explained Simply**

Algorithmic trading uses computer programs to follow specific rules:

-   If price crosses moving average → Buy
-   If RSI is overbought → Sell
-   If volatility increases → Adjust lot size

This removes emotional trading, which is often a trader’s biggest weakness.
