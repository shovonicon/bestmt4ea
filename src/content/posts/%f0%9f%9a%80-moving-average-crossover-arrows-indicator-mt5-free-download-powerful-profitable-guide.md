## **What Is a Moving Average Crossover?**

A moving average crossover happens when two moving averages intersect on a chart.

### **Basic Concept**

-   A **fast moving average** reacts quickly to price changes.
-   A **slow moving average** reacts more gradually.
-   When the fast MA crosses above the slow MA → **Buy signal**
-   When the fast MA crosses below the slow MA → **Sell signal**

Simple, right?
