## **What is Volume Profile?**

Volume profile is an advanced charting tool that shows how much volume was traded at specific price levels during a selected time period. Instead of displaying volume as vertical bars below the chart, it displays horizontal bars along the price axis.

This gives traders a clearer view of where institutional traders are active.

In platforms like MetaTrader 4 (MT4), volume profile isn’t included by default. That’s why many traders search for the **Best Volume Profile Indicator for MT4 FREE Download** to gain this advantage.

## **Why Volume Profile is Superior to Standard Volume**

Traditional volume only tells you how much activity occurred during a candle.

Volume profile tells you:

-   Where buyers and sellers are positioned
-   Where price is likely to stall
-   High liquidity zones
-   Institutional accumulation areas

That’s a game changer.

# **How to Install Volume Profile Indicator in MT4**

## **Step-by-Step Installation Guide**

2.  Open MT4
3.  Click File → Open Data Folder
4.  Go to MQL4 → Indicators
5.  Paste file
6.  Restart MT4
7.  Drag indicator onto chart

Done!

## **Common Installation Errors and Fixes**

-   Indicator not showing → Enable DLL imports
-   Compilation error → Recompile in MetaEditor

# **How to Use Volume Profile Effectively in Trading**

## **Identifying Point of Control (POC)**

POC = Price with highest traded volume.

Traders often:

-   Use it as magnet price
-   Expect price reaction
-   Place entries near retests

## **Trading High Volume Nodes (HVN)**

HVNs indicate fair value areas.

Price tends to:

-   Consolidate here
-   Reverse here

Great for range trading.

## **Using Low Volume Nodes (LVN) for Breakouts**

LVNs show rejection zones.

Price often:

-   Moves quickly through LVNs
-   Breaks aggressively

Excellent breakout trigger.
