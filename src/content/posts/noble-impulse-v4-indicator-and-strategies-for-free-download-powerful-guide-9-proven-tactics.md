## **Signal Confirmation Strategy (Beginner Friendly)**

This is the “don’t just click because an arrow appears” plan.

### **Trend Filter Using a Moving Average**

Use one moving average (MA) as a trend line:

-   If price is **above** the MA, you only look for buys
-   If price is **below** the MA, you only look for sells

You’re not trying to predict tops and bottoms—you’re trying to avoid trading against momentum.

### **Candle Confirmation Rule**

After a signal appears:

-   Wait for the candle to close
-   Enter only if the candle closes in the signal direction (bullish close for buy, bearish close for sell)

This reduces impulsive entries on spikes.

## **Support/Resistance Zone Strategy (High-Probability Entries)**

Noble Impulse V4 promotes adjustable support/resistance zones as part of the toolkit.  
Here are two practical ways to [trade with zones](https://bestmt4ea.com/ict-kill-zones-indicator-mt4-mt5-free-download-powerful-proven-2025-trading-edge/ "ICT Kill Zones Indicator MT4/MT5: Free Download & Trading Guide").

### **Bounce Trades**

Use zones like “decision areas.”

1.  Signal appears near a support zone (for buys) or resistance zone (for sells)
2.  Wait for confirmation (candle close rule)
3.  Place stop loss beyond the zone (a bit of breathing room)
4.  Target the next zone or a fixed risk-to-reward

### **Break-and-Retest Trades**

This is a safer breakout approach:

1.  Price breaks a zone clearly
2.  Wait for price to **retest** the broken zone
3.  Enter with the trend after confirmation
4.  Stop goes on the other side of the zone

## **Risk Management That Keeps You Alive**

If you skip this part, the “best” indicator won’t save you.

### **Simple Stop-Loss Placement Methods**

Pick one:

-   **Swing method:** stop below the last swing low (for buys) / above last swing high (for sells)
-   **Zone method:** stop beyond the support/resistance zone you used
-   **Volatility method (ATR-style idea):** wider stops in high volatility, tighter in low volatility

### **Take-Profit Frameworks**

Two easy frameworks:

-   **Fixed R:R:** aim for 1:1.5 or 1:2 (risk 10 pips to make 15–20)
-   **Scale out:** take partial profit at 1R, move stop to breakeven, let the rest run

Also consider a **daily max loss** (example: stop for the day after 2 losses). It prevents revenge trading.

## **Backtesting and Forward Testing (Don’t Skip This)**

Before risking real money:

-   Backtest at least 50–100 trades on your chosen timeframe
-   Forward test on demo for 2–4 weeks

Track:

-   Win rate
-   Average win vs average loss
-   Drawdown
-   Best and worst market conditions (trending vs ranging)

This is how you learn whether your settings and strategy actually fit your style.

## **Common Mistakes and How to Fix Them**

-   **Taking every signal:** add trend + candle confirmation
-   **Trading during major news spikes:** pause around high-impact events
-   **Using one setting for all pairs:** adjust sensitivity per instrument volatility
-   **Ignoring spread/fees:** especially on lower timeframes
-   **No stop loss:** one big move can erase weeks of gains
