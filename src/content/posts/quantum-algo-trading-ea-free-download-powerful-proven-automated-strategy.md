An Expert Advisor is essentially a trading robot coded in MQL (MetaQuotes Language). It can:

-   Manage stop-loss and take-profit levels

The beauty of EAs is that they remove emotional decision-making. No fear. No greed. Just rules.

## **How Algorithmic Trading Works**

Algorithmic trading uses mathematical models and statistical analysis to identify market patterns. A typical EA may rely on:

-   Moving averages
-   RSI (Relative Strength Index)
-   Bollinger Bands
-   Price action patterns
-   Volatility filters

When predefined conditions are met, the EA triggers a trade.

For example:

-   If RSI < 30 → Buy
-   If RSI > 70 → Sell

But advanced systems like Quantum-style EAs often combine multiple indicators for higher accuracy.

## **Installation Guide for MetaTrader Platforms**

### **Installing on MetaTrader 4**

1.  Download the EA file (.ex4 or .mq4)
2.  Open MT4
3.  Click **File → Open Data Folder**
4.  Go to **MQL4 → Experts**
5.  Paste the file
6.  Restart MT4
7.  Drag the EA onto your chart
8.  Enable “AutoTrading”

### **Installing on MetaTrader 5**

Steps are similar but inside the **MQL5 → Experts** folder.

Always test the EA on a demo account first.
