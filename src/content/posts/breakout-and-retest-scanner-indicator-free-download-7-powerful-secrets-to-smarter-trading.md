## **Introduction to Breakout and Retest Trading Strategy**

If you’re searching for the **Breakout and Retest Scanner Indicator FREE Download**, you’re likely looking to improve your trading accuracy and timing. Good news—you’re on the right path. Breakout and retest trading is one of the most powerful and widely used technical analysis strategies in the financial markets.

This strategy focuses on identifying when price breaks through a significant support or resistance level and then returns (or “retests”) that level before continuing in the breakout direction. Traders love it because it provides confirmation, reducing false entries.

Let’s break it down in simple terms.

### **What Is a Breakout in Trading?**

A breakout happens when the price moves beyond a defined support or resistance level with strong momentum. Imagine price has been bouncing between two levels for days. Suddenly, it bursts above resistance. That’s a breakout.

Breakouts signal potential trend continuation and often come with increased volume.

### **What Does Retest Mean in Technical Analysis?**

After the breakout, price frequently pulls back to the level it just broke. This is the retest. If the old resistance now acts as support (or vice versa), it confirms strength in the move.

Smart traders wait for this retest before entering a trade. Why? Because it reduces the risk of fake breakouts.
