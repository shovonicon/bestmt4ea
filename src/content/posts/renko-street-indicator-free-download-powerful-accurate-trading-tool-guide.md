## What Is the Renko Street Indicator?

The **Renko Street Indicator** is a trading tool that builds Renko-style price bricks directly on your trading platform. Unlike traditional candlestick charts that move with time, Renko charts move only when price changes by a fixed amount.

That’s the secret sauce.

If price doesn’t move enough, no new brick forms. This removes small price fluctuations (market noise) and makes trends easier to spot.

### Why Traders Prefer Renko Charts

Renko charts are popular because they:

-   Filter minor price movements
-   Highlight strong trends
-   Reduce emotional trading
-   Make support and resistance clearer
-   Help improve risk management

In simple terms, they help you focus on what truly matters — price direction.

* * *

## How Renko Charts Work

Renko charts are built using bricks (or blocks). Each brick represents a fixed price movement.

For example:

-   If you set the brick size to 10 pips
-   A new brick forms only when price moves 10 pips
-   If price reverses 10 pips, a brick forms in the opposite direction

There is no time factor involved.

### Traditional Candlestick vs Renko Chart

| Feature | Candlestick Chart | Renko Chart |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Noise Level | High | Low |
| Trend Clarity | Moderate | High |

Renko gives you a smoother view of market direction.
