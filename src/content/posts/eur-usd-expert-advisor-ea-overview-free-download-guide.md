## How to Install EURUSD EA on MT4 (Step-by-Step)

4

Follow these steps:

1.  Download the EA (.ex4 or .mq4 file)
2.  Open MT4 → Click **File** → Open Data Folder
3.  Go to: MQL4 → Experts
4.  Paste the EA file inside
5.  Restart MT4
6.  Drag the EA onto EUR/USD chart
7.  Enable “AutoTrading”

That’s it! Your EA will now start trading automatically.

## Hidden Risks of Free Forex Robots

Before downloading any EURUSD EA FREE Download file, understand the risks:

### 🚨 Martingale Strategy

Many free EAs double lot sizes after losses. This can wipe your account quickly.

### 🚨 Over-Optimized Backtests

Some developers curve-fit their EA to historical data. It looks amazing in backtests but fails in live markets.

### 🚨 No Risk Management

Some bots trade without proper stop-loss protection.
