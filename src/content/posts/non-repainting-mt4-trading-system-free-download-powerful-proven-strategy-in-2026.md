## **Understanding [Non-Repainting Indicators](https://bestmt4ea.com/best-non-repainting-arrow-indicator-for-forex/ "non-repainting indicators")**

### **How Repainting Indicators Work**

Repainting indicators adjust past signals based on new price data. For example:

-   A buy arrow appears on a candle.
-   After the next candle forms, the arrow disappears.
-   The signal shifts to a new position.

This creates a false impression of accuracy in backtests. When looking at historical charts, everything appears perfect. But in live trading? Not so much.

[Repainting happens because some indicators](https://bestmt4ea.com/supply-and-demand-indicator-for-mt4-no-repaint/ "Supply and Demand Indicator for MT4: No Repaint") calculate values using future data. That’s a serious problem for real-world trading.

### **Why Non-Repainting Systems Matter**

Non-repainting indicators lock their signals once a candle closes. That means:

-   No signal shifting.
-   No disappearing arrows.
-   Clear historical accuracy.
-   Realistic backtesting results.

This reliability is what makes a non-repainting trading system valuable. You see what truly happened, not a polished illusion.
