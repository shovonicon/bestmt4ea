## 📊 What Is the CPR Forex Indicator?

4

The **Central Pivot Range (CPR)** is a technical analysis tool based on pivot points. It consists of three primary levels:

-   **Pivot Point (PP)**
-   **Top Central Pivot (TC)**
-   **Bottom Central Pivot (BC)**

These levels are calculated using the previous day’s high, low, and closing prices.

The CPR indicator is widely used in platforms like MetaTrader 4 (MT4) because it integrates easily and offers real-time visual analysis.

* * *

## 🔍 How CPR Is Calculated

The formulas behind CPR are simple:

-   **Pivot Point (PP)** = (High + Low + Close) ÷ 3
-   **Bottom Central (BC)** = (High + Low) ÷ 2
-   **Top Central (TC)** = (PP − BC) + PP

Although the math is straightforward, the real magic lies in how traders interpret the width and position of the CPR range.
