# **Introduction to Harmonic Trading**

## **What Are Harmonic Patterns?**

Harmonic patterns are price formations that use Fibonacci ratios to predict potential market reversals. Unlike random chart patterns, harmonic patterns rely on mathematical precision. They follow specific Fibonacci retracement and extension levels, making them structured and reliable.

These patterns form geometric shapes on the price chart and signal areas where price may reverse.

## **Why Harmonic Trading Works in Forex**

The Forex market is driven by repetition and psychology. Price moves in waves, and Fibonacci ratios naturally appear in these movements. Harmonic patterns combine geometry and Fibonacci to create high-probability setups.
