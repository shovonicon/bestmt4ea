## What Is a Fair Value Gap (FVG)?

A **Fair Value Gap (FVG)** is a price imbalance that forms when the market moves aggressively in one direction, leaving a gap between candle wicks. This typically happens during:

-   High volatility events
-   Institutional order flow
-   Breakouts
-   News releases

### How It Forms

An FVG is usually identified using a three-candle pattern:

1.  A strong impulsive candle
2.  A continuation candle
3.  A gap between the first and third candle

When price leaves an imbalance, it often returns later to “fill” that gap before continuing in the original direction.

### Why FVG Matters

Institutional traders (smart money) create these imbalances. Retail traders can use FVG zones to:

-   Identify high-probability retracement entries
-   Avoid chasing impulsive moves
-   Time precise limit orders

Simply put, FVG zones act as magnets for price.

## What Is a Balanced Price Range (BPR)?

Balanced Price Range (BPR) occurs when two opposing [Fair Value Gaps](https://bestmt4ea.com/%f0%9f%93%8a-fair-value-gap-signals-by-ualgo-forex-indicator-reviews-the-ultimate-honest-breakdown-for-smart-traders/ "Fair Value Gap Indicator Review: UAlgo FVG Signals Tested") overlap — one bullish and one bearish — creating an equilibrium zone.

This area represents:

-   Temporary market balance
-   Liquidity consolidation
-   Potential breakout zone

### Why BPR Is Powerful

Balanced Price Range zones often:

-   Act as strong support or resistance
-   Signal accumulation or distribution
-   Lead to explosive breakouts

When price returns to a BPR, it usually reacts strongly.
