## Trading involves substantial risk

Trading foreign exchange, CFDs, commodities and cryptocurrencies is high risk. Leverage
magnifies both gains and losses. **You can lose some or all of the money you deposit.**
Never trade money you cannot afford to lose.

Nothing on this website is financial, investment, legal or tax advice, and nothing here
is a personal recommendation. We publish software, configuration material and research.

## Performance figures

Every performance figure on this site is taken from a third-party tracking account and
links to its source so you can verify it yourself. Please read those figures with care:

- **Past performance does not indicate future results.** A historical curve is a record
  of what happened, not a forecast.
- **Backtests are not live trading.** Simulated results cannot reproduce real fills,
  slippage, spreads or liquidity.
- **Account type is always shown.** Where a tracked account holds demo funds rather than
  live money, the page states that plainly. A demo account is not a live result.
- **Results are specific.** They belong to one broker, one account type and one settings
  file. Your results will differ, and can be worse.
