## The short version

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you open one and sign up or buy, we may
earn a commission. **It costs you nothing extra** — you pay the same price you would
have paid going direct.

## How you can tell

- Affiliate links open with `rel="sponsored nofollow noopener"`.
- Pages that carry them show a disclosure line before the first one.
- Any affiliate call-to-action is kept **away from download buttons**, so a commercial
  link can never be mistaken for a free download.

## What we earn from

- **Broker links** — if you open an account with a broker through our link, the broker
  may pay us a referral fee.
- **Product and service links** — VPS providers and similar trading infrastructure.
- **Advertising** — display ads served by Google AdSense.
- **Our own products** — sales of BESTMT4EA licences, where the link is to our own
  store rather than an affiliate.

## What this does not change

- **Reviews stay honest.** We publish drawdown beside gain, state when an account is
  demo rather than live, and say when a system is unsuitable. A commission is never a
  reason to soften that.
- **Rankings are not for sale.** Where we rank systems, the order is driven by the
  published performance data and the criteria stated on the page.
- **No affiliate link is placed next to a download.** Free downloads are free, and
  nothing commercial is attached to them.
- **You are never obliged to use our links.** If you prefer, go to the provider
  directly — the price and the product are the same.

## What it means for your data

Following an affiliate link takes you to the partner's own site. That site sets its own
cookie so the referral can be credited — it belongs to them, not to us, and we do not
receive your account details from it. The [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/) explains
what this site, our host and our advertising and affiliate partners do with data, and how
to limit it.
