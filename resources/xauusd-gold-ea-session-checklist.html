XAUUSD (Gold) EA Session & Settings Checklist

Gold is not a currency pair with a different name. XAUUSD moves further per hour, gaps harder around US data, and punishes settings copied from EURUSD. Run this checklist before any gold EA trades — on demo or live.

1. Know your broker's gold contract

  • Contract size: 1 standard lot is usually 100 oz. A 0.01 lot still moves about $1 per $1 move in gold — check, do not assume.
  • Digits: most brokers quote 2 or 3 decimals. A "30 point" stop means 0.30 on a 2-digit quote and 0.030 on a 3-digit quote. This one setting breaks more gold EAs than any other.
  • Spread at your session: record the typical spread at London open, New York open, and the Asian session. An EA backtested at 20 points dies at a live spread of 45.
  • Swap: gold swaps are heavy. A scalper holding overnight can pay more in swap than it targets in profit.

2. Session rules

  • Highest-quality movement: London open and the London/New York overlap.
  • Highest-risk minutes: US CPI, FOMC, NFP and payroll Fridays — decide in advance whether the EA pauses or trades, and write it down.
  • Avoid the daily rollover window (about 21:00–23:00 GMT, broker-dependent): spreads widen several-fold for a few minutes.
  • Friday: gold frequently trends into the close; grid and martingale EAs should reduce exposure or stop opening new baskets before the weekend gap.
