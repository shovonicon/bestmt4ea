EA Troubleshooting Field Guide: Log Errors Decoded

When an expert advisor misbehaves, the terminal already knows why — the answer is sitting in the Experts and Journal logs as a code or a short line of text. This field guide translates the messages you will actually see and gives the fix in the order to try it.

1. Find the real message first

  • Open the Experts tab (and Journal) at the bottom of the terminal. The first error after the EA was attached is the cause; later lines are usually consequences.
  • Note the exact time. Match it against news releases, rollover, and your VPS restart schedule before changing any setting.
  • Change one thing, then re-test. Five changes at once tells you nothing about which one worked.

2. The messages, decoded

Log messageMeaningFix order
Error 130 — invalid stopsStop loss or take profit is closer than the broker's minimum distance.Check the broker's stop level for the symbol; widen stops or let the EA's stop buffer input handle it.
Error 131 — invalid trade volumeLot size breaks the broker's min/max/step rules.Round lots to the broker's step (usually 0.01); check the EA's auto-lot maths on small balances.
Error 133 — trade is disabledThe symbol or account does not allow trading right now.Check market hours, suffix symbols, and whether the account is investor-password logged in.
Error 138 — requotePrice moved while the order was being placed (MT4).Raise deviation/slippage allowance modestly; check VPS latency; avoid rollover minutes.
Error 146 — trade context busyTwo EAs or scripts are giving orders at the same instant (MT4).One EA per chart is fine; avoid multiple EAs trading the same symbol on one terminal.
Error 4109 — trade is not allowedAutoTrading is off or the EA's chart permission is missing.Enable Algo/AutoTrading globally and in the EA's attach dialog.
Retcode 10027 (MT5) — autotrading disabledSame family as 4109, MT5 wording.Toolbar Algo Trading button + Tools → Options → Expert Advisors.
Retcode 10018 — market closedOrder sent outside the symbol's sessions.Check the symbol's session table in Specification; gold and indices close daily.

3. The demo-works-live-doesn't checklist

  • Different spreads: the demo feed is often tighter. Record live spreads at your trading hours.
  • Different execution: demo fills are instant; live fills slip, especially in the first minute of a session.
  • Different account mode: an EA written for hedging accounts misbehaves on MT5 netting accounts.
  • Different symbol properties: swap, stops level and contract size differ between demo and live servers more often than vendors admit.
The field rule: never fix a live problem by increasing risk. If the cure for an error is a bigger lot, a wider grid or a removed stop, the error was not the real problem.
