News Trading Methods Cheat Sheet: 9 Ways to Protect an EA

Every EA owner eventually asks the same question: how do I stop it trading through the news? There are nine honest methods, and they differ in cost, reliability and how much of the calendar they cover. This sheet compares them so you can pick deliberately.

The nine methods, compared

#MethodCostStrengthWeakness
1Manual pause: you switch AutoTrading off before releasesFreeTotal controlYou must be awake, every time, forever
2EA built-in news filter input (minutes before/after)Free if presentAutomatic, per-EADepends on the EA's calendar source being right
3Third-party news filter indicator/EA add-onFree–paidWorks with EAs that lack filtersAnother moving part; server time offsets
4Calendar via WebRequest (EA reads an online calendar)FreeLive dataNeeds URL whitelist + internet; breaks silently if the feed changes
5Fixed time blocks: same release times weekly/monthly (e.g. US CPI day)FreeSimple, robustMisses unscheduled events and time changes
6Spread gate: no entries when spread exceeds XFreeCatches surprise events tooDoesn't close existing positions; spikes can precede the gate
7Equity/drawdown guard: halt all trading at a daily loss capFree–paidLimits damage from every cause, not just newsReactive — damage up to the cap still happens
8Close-all before the event (flatten, don't just pause)FreeNo open exposure through the spikePays spread to exit and re-enter; can crystallise losses
9Broker-side: choose not to run the EA on data days (session rules in the EA)FreeRemoves whole risk daysBlunt: skips some perfectly good days

Picking your stack

  • Minimum serious stack: method 2 or 3 (entries blocked) + method 7 (damage capped). One prevents, one limits.
  • If your EA holds positions for days, add method 8's decision explicitly: flatten or hold — but decide before the calendar does.
  • Whatever you pick, test it once on demo through a real red-folder release and read the log. An untested filter is a hope with a settings page.
  • Write your server-time offset on this sheet: most "the filter didn't work" stories are a two-hour offset wearing a disguise.
The cheat-sheet rule: news protection fails in layers or not at all. A method that blocks entries but lets a basket ride the spike has protected your future trades and abandoned your current ones.
