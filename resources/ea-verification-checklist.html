EA Verification Checklist: Prove Any Expert Advisor Before You Buy

Every EA listing shows the same things: an equity curve, a win rate, and a price. None of those prove anything by themselves. This checklist is the evidence sequence to run on any robot — from any vendor, including us — before money changes hands.

1. The track record

  • The results link goes to an independent tracker (Myfxbook, FX Blue or similar) — not a screenshot, not the vendor's own page.
  • The account is fully verified: track record verified AND trading privileges verified. One without the other proves half as much.
  • The account is real money, or the listing says plainly that it is a demo. Demo results are rehearsal, not evidence.
  • The history is open: closed trades visible, not hidden behind "custom analysis" date ranges that start after a drawdown.
  • The record is longer than 6 months and includes at least one ugly month. A curve with no bad month is a curve with hidden months.

2. The risk numbers that matter

NumberWhere to find itWhat you want to see
Maximum drawdownTracker's equity (not balance) curveA figure you could personally sit through, stated in money on your account size
Drawdown durationLongest peak-to-recovery stretchWeeks you can tolerate, not months you cannot
Average loss vs average winTrade statisticsIf losses are 3× wins, the win rate must be very high — do the arithmetic
Worst single dayDaily historySmaller than your written daily loss limit
Lot sizing patternOpen trades / historyFixed or % based. Increasing lots after losses = martingale, whatever the sales page calls it

3. The strategy questions to ask the vendor

  • Does it use martingale, grid, hedging or averaging down? A refusal to answer is an answer.
  • What market condition hurts it most — trend, range, news, low volatility? Every real strategy has one.
  • Which pairs and timeframes was it actually developed on, and what happens outside them?
  • What is the refund policy, in writing, and has anyone you can find actually received one?
The verification rule: claims you cannot check are marketing, not information. Price your decision on the evidence column of this sheet — if a row is blank, the EA has not earned that part of its price.
