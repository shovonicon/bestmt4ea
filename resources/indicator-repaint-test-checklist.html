Indicator Repaint & Selection Test Checklist

An indicator that repaints shows you a history that never existed: signals that look perfect on a closed chart were moving, appearing and disappearing while the candle was live. This checklist tests any indicator for repainting and scores it before it earns a place in a strategy.

1. The repaint test (30 minutes, demo chart)

  • Attach the indicator to a live demo chart on a low timeframe (M1 or M5) where repainting is easiest to catch.
  • Take a screenshot the moment a signal appears, with the time visible.
  • Wait for the candle to close and for two more candles to form. Screenshot the same moment again.
  • Compare: did the signal move, change size, or vanish? Any change after the candle closed is repainting, whatever the sales page calls it ("recalculation", "optimisation").
  • Repeat with the terminal's strategy tester in visual mode: signals must appear at the same bars in the tester and on the live chart.

3. Selection scorecard

  • It answers one question well (trend, momentum, levels, volatility) instead of promising all four.
  • It agrees with price more often than it argues with it: if you need three filters to make it usable, it is not the tool.
  • You can explain, in one sentence, what would make its signal wrong. If nothing can, it is decoration.
  • Two weeks of forward screenshots before it influences a single trade decision.
The repaint rule: judge an indicator only by what it showed at the moment of decision. A signal you could not have seen in real time was never a signal.
