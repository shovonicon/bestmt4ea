Prop Firm EA Risk Rules Worksheet

Prop firm challenges are not lost by bad entries. They are lost by breaking a written rule on a bad day: the daily loss limit, the overall drawdown limit, or a consistency requirement. Fill this worksheet in for your firm before the EA places a trade — the numbers differ per firm, and the EA must be set to the strictest one.

2. Set the EA inside the rules, not at them

  • Daily stop in the EA = 60–70% of the firm's daily limit. The firm's limit includes floating losses and fees; the EA's stop must trigger first.
  • Overall equity protection = stop all trading at 70–80% of the max drawdown. A trailing limit moves with your balance — recalculate the money value weekly.
  • Risk per trade: divide the daily budget by the number of trades you allow per day. If that gives a lot size that feels boring, the maths is doing its job.
  • One strategy, one account: running two uncorrelated EAs doubles your worst day, not your safety.

3. Daily routine

  • Before the session: write today's loss budget in money. When it is gone, the EA comes off — no override.
  • Check the calendar: if the firm restricts news trading, the EA's news filter must be on and tested.
  • After the session: record best/worst trade and floating drawdown. Consistency rules punish one giant winning day as much as a losing one.
Reality check: a martingale or grid EA with no hard equity stop cannot be made prop-firm safe by lowering the lot size. The first deep basket will find the daily limit. If the EA has no equity-stop input, it is the wrong tool for a challenge.
